Our Breakthrough

The Membraneless
Electrolyzer

The first and only membraneless electrolyzer designed and manufactured in Thailand — cutting green hydrogen production costs by more than 2× vs any market alternative.

01
No Membrane Required
We eliminate the most expensive component entirely — through innovative flow-cell design that separates gases without a costly polymer membrane.
02
5× Cheaper Materials
Our electrode materials are 5× cheaper than conventional technology with only 5–10% efficiency trade-off — a commercially decisive advantage.
03
Zero-Carbon Combustion
Produced from water + renewable electricity: 2H₂O → O₂ + 2H₂. Burns to emit only water vapour — genuinely zero-carbon at point of use.
04
Modular, Scalable, Drop-in
Stack units seamlessly as demand grows. Compatible with existing industrial burners at under 10% blend — no equipment changes needed.
